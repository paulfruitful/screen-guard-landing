Features

Built for real-world protection

Simple controls for everyday users, powered by fast on-device detection.

Strict Mode Always On

No confusing sensitivity toggles or temporary bypasses. Screen Guard enforces consistent, strict filtering whenever explicit imagery or video appears.

Fast Social Feed Detection

Analyzes in-feed videos and posts in real time so explicit content is covered immediately without slowing down your scrolling.

Auto-Fade Recovery

Once you scroll past or close explicit content, the protective shield automatically fades away so you can continue browsing normally.

Scripture Encouragement

Each intervention shares a randomly chosen Bible verse to encourage purity, with one tap to open the full passage in your Bible app or on bible.com.

Return to Safe Browsing

One tap closes the offending screen in the background and brings you directly back to the Screen Guard Home dashboard.

Trusted App Exclusions

Choose trusted apps like WhatsApp, Messages, or Gmail in Settings whenever you want Screen Guard to skip them.

How It Works

Three quiet steps in the background

01

Observes On-Screen Frames

Uses Android Accessibility safely to inspect the active screen while automatically skipping keyboards, passwords, and private windows.

02

Checks Locally with On-Device AI

Evaluates frames inside temporary device memory in milliseconds — nothing is saved to storage or sent to any server.

03

Shields & Encourages

Covers explicit content with the Screen Guard shield, offers a strengthening Bible verse, and fades away when the screen is safe.

Privacy Promise

100% Private on Your Phone

Screen Guard was built from the ground up so your personal browsing never leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud servers, and zero saved screenshots.

  • Zero cloud uploads or external API calls
  • No screenshots written to disk or gallery
  • Skips password fields and banking/incognito windows
  • Open source on GitHub for full transparency
Get Started

Up and running in under a minute

1

Install the APK

Download ScreenGuard-v1.3.3.apk on your Android 11+ phone and install it.

2

Enable Accessibility

Open Screen Guard, tap Enable Protection, and switch Screen Guard Content Observer ON.

3

Keep Alive in Background

Allow background protection if prompted so your phone keeps the shield active at all times.
